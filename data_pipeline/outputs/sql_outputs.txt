query_6_join_top_rated
================================================================================
SQL:
SELECT
            c.category_name,
            b.title,
            b.rating,
            b.price_gbp,
            b.price_inr,
            b.in_stock
        FROM books b
        INNER JOIN categories c
            ON b.category_id = c.category_id
        ORDER BY
            c.category_name ASC,
            b.rating DESC,
            b.price_gbp DESC,
            b.price_inr DESC,
            b.title ASC,
            b.in_stock ASC;
